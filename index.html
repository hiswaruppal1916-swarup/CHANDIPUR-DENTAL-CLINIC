CHANDIPUR DENTAL CLINIC — An Unit Of Renuka Physiotherapy Center

Dr. N.D. Maity is committed to delivering accessible, reliable, and patient-centered dental care to the community of Chandipur, West Bengal.

Operating as an integral unit of the Renuka Physiotherapy Center, the clinic combines clinical precision with a warm, reassuring environment designed to alleviate dental anxiety for every patient.

Our Patient Philosophy:

  • Active Listening: Taking the time to understand each patient's specific concerns before recommending care.

  • Transparent Advice: Honest treatment options tailored to patient needs without unnecessary procedures.
